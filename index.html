My Dearest Carvie,

I don't really know why I'm writing this anymore.

Maybe because there are still things I never learned how to say when you were here.

I thought that with enough time, you'd eventually become just another memory. Someone I used to know. Someone whose name wouldn't make my chest feel a little heavier.

But somehow, you stayed.

Not beside me. Not in my life anymore. Just somewhere in the quiet parts of me that I can't seem to reach.

I miss you, Carvie.

I don't expect you to come back. I don't want this letter to make you feel guilty. I just wanted these words to exist somewhere, because they've existed inside me for so long.

Maybe someday, I'll stop looking for pieces of you in places you no longer belong.

I hope you're doing okay.

— Adamm
